FAQ

Straight answers, before you commit to anything.

How does the deposit work?+

Every package starts with a small deposit — $250 for the Website Launch, $125 for the Rescue, $150 for the Landing Page Sprint, less for the extras. It books your job, it is credited in full toward the fixed price, and it is refundable until work starts. If you change your mind before we begin, you get it back.

When is the balance due?+

Only when the work is delivered and you have approved it — for a website, when the site is live and you are happy with it. The balance is never invoiced before that point.

How fast will my site be ready?+

Days, not months. These are focused builds with a fixed scope, not open-ended projects. Once your deposit lands and we have what we need from you, the build moves in a single sprint, and we confirm the delivery window in writing before work starts.

What do you need from me?+

The basics about your business: your services, the areas you cover, and how customers should reach you. A logo and photos help if you have them — send what exists. If you do not have them, we can work without them and keep the design clean.

What happens after I send the form?+

We read it and reply by email, usually the same day, with your fixed price confirmed in writing and a deposit invoice attached to the reply. Pay the deposit by card or PayPal whenever you are ready — work starts the moment it lands. No payment is taken from the form itself.

Who owns my website?+

You do. The site, the domain, and the content are yours outright.
