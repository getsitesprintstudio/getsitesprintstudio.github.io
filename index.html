How every job works

One clear process, in writing.

Intake

Answer 8 questions on one short form. No call required — everything runs in writing.

Fixed quote

You get a written quote restating the exact scope, exclusions, delivery window, and revision limit at one fixed price.

Deposit books the job

The deposit is credited in full toward the package price and is refundable until the build start date confirmed in writing; after build start it covers the work already scheduled.

Build

The work is built in a preview environment and checked on phone, tablet, and desktop sizes. Revision rounds are included per package.

The remaining balance is due only before the work goes live on your domain. You own the site outright.

Standard exclusions (all packages): no e-commerce builds, no custom web apps, no logo/brand design, no copywriting from scratch (we clean up and structure text you supply — facts come from you, nothing is invented), no hosting or domain purchases on your behalf, no work that requires live calls or meetings.
