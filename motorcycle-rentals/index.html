Renting Out Your Motorcycles

Hi Dad,

I looked into your idea of renting out the motorcycles.

Here is the short version.

You already own the bikes. You already own the trucks to deliver them.

That is the expensive part. And it is already done.

— Peter

The idea

You list your bikes for rent.

People visiting Yosemite and Bass Lake rent them for a day or a few days.

You deliver the bike to them. They ride. You pick it up.

No storefront needed.

What it costs to start

About $1,300 to $2,000.

That is mostly helmets and basic supplies.

That is it to get going, if you start the simple way.

What you could make

About $3,000 a month in a normal, middle case.

Some months less. Some months more.

  • Slow pace: about $1,500 a month
  • Middle pace: about $3,000 a month
  • Busy pace: about $5,550 a month

Summer will be your best time. Winter will be slower.

These numbers are an average over the whole year.

The simple way to start

Use a company called Riders Share.

They do the hard parts:

  • They find the renters
  • They check licenses
  • They take the payment
  • They insure each trip

You keep about 75% of your price.

You just list the bikes, deliver them, and check them when they come back.

You only need about 3 rental days a month, across all 10 bikes, to break even.

At the middle pace, you make your startup money back in about one month.

You can go fully on your own later if you want. That can make about $1,800 a month in the middle case. But it costs about $850 a month for your own insurance, even in slow months. So start simple first. Learn the demand for one season. Then decide.

Who else is doing this?

Nobody near you.

There is no motorcycle rental in North Fork. None in Bass Lake. None in Oakhurst.

Your closest competition is in Fresno, about 55 miles away.

EagleRider Fresno

  • Rents Harleys only
  • $124 a day on special. Usually $150 to $250+ a day
  • You have to go to them. They do not deliver.

That is your opening. You are close. They are not. And you deliver.

Proof people will pay:
A company in Oakhurst has been open since 2015. They charge $399 to $1,199 for guided UTV tours.

A full day on one of your bikes would be $95 to $140. That is cheaper than their 2-hour tour.

And Yosemite had 4.28 million visitors in 2025. You are about 14 miles from the south entrance.

In one line: You would be the closest motorcycle rental to Yosemite's south entrance, and the only one who brings the bike to where people are staying.

Who you need to contact

In this order. You do not need to call everyone at once.

1. Riders Share
Make a free owner account. List the bikes there.

2. Madera County
Ask about a business license. The free state site CalGold — calgold.ca.gov — will tell you what you need for North Fork.

3. The IRS
Get a free EIN number online. It takes a few minutes.

4. A local CPA
One short talk, about 30 minutes. Ask about taxes, and whether to start simple or set up an LLC.

5. Google
Set up a free Google Business Profile. That is how tourists will find you.

6. The hotels and resorts
Go say hello in person:

  • The hotels in Oakhurst
  • The resorts at Bass Lake
  • Tenaya Lodge at Fish Camp
  • The campgrounds and the visitor center

Your pitch is simple: "We deliver motorcycles to your guests."

What you need to decide

  1. Do you want to start on Riders Share for the first season?
  2. Which bikes are you willing to rent out? Are any off-limits?
  3. How far will you deliver?

That is all you need to decide to start.

Full competition list

  • North Fork, Bass Lake, Oakhurst: No street motorcycle rental found. Only boats, UTVs, Jeeps, and e-bikes.
  • EagleRider Fresno: 2577 N Fowler Ave. 13 Harley models. $124 a day specials in 2026. Normal touring $150–$250+ a day. No delivery to Bass Lake or Oakhurst.
  • Riders Share near you: One bike in Clovis at $60 a day. No bikes listed in North Fork, Bass Lake, Oakhurst, or Coarsegold.
  • Twisted Road: No listings found near Fresno or Oakhurst.
  • Yosemite Adventure Company, Oakhurst: Open since 2015. UTV tours $749–$1,199. A 2-hour Bass Lake tour is about $399–$506 per vehicle.
  • Season: Best June to September. Good April, May, and October. Slow November to March. Yosemite stays open all year.

Risks, plainly

  • Bikes will get dropped and scratched. That is normal. Take clear photos before and after every rental.
  • On a platform, damage cover is up to $30,000 per bike.
  • Never rent a bike for cash off the platform. Then there is no insurance at all.
  • Fire and smoke can hurt a season. The Creek Fire reached Bass Lake in 2020.
  • Yosemite entry rules can change. That is another reason to keep costs low in year one.

Starting steps, in full

  1. Start on a platform.
  2. County license check, free EIN, and the CPA talk. An LLC costs $70 to set up, plus $800 a year in California tax. You may also need a free seller's permit, because rentals are usually taxed.
  3. Check all 10 bikes: tires, brakes, fluids, lights. Take photos of every bike from every side.
  4. Buy helmets. California law says every rider and passenger needs a DOT helmet. Budget about $800–$1,200 for a set of sizes.
  5. List the bikes at $95–$140 a day. Offer delivery: free close by, about $49 to Oakhurst, Bass Lake, and Fish Camp, and about $99 or more for Fresno or the airport.
  6. Set up the Google profile, then visit the hotels and resorts.

If you go on your own later

  • MBA Insurance: Get a live quote for your bikes.
  • A California attorney: Have them check your rental agreement before you use it.
