01 — The idea

One empire.
One front door.

Until now, every Cubana venue lived on its own page, with its own number, on its own island. This platform ends that.

Fifteen venues. Five cities. Until today, fifteen different ways to reach them — and no single place that held them all.

Cubana Group's own website is gone, and each club, lounge, grill and hotel has been left to run itself — a Facebook page here, a different reservation number there. Guests guess. Tables sit unbooked while a phone rings in another city.

This site is the fix, built the way the group itself is built: bold, after dark, and impossible to miss. One directory of every venue. One reservation flow for every table. One booking flow for Grand Cubana Hotels. And behind all of it, a single WhatsApp line that answers for the whole empire.

05 — The story

From one club
to an empire.

Obinna Iyiegbu — Obi Cubana — opened a single Abuja lounge in 2006. Twenty years on, his name is the night, in five cities.

2006

Ibiza, Abuja

Obi Cubana opens Ibiza Club in Abuja — the dance lounge he calls the mother of Cubana, and the seed of everything on this page.

2009

Cubana is founded, Owerri

The first full Cubana hospitality club opens in Owerri, Imo State — the city that still carries the group's name over its gate.

LAGOS

Adeola Odeku & Ikeja

Lagos gets the full house: Pablo and The Grind on Victoria Island, Montana in GRA Ikeja, and Cubana Hut & Grill feeding both sides of the city.
