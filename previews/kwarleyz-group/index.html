01 — The Group

One group.
Five companies.

The developments had their own pages. The group did not have a front door. This platform is that front door — every venture, one enquiry route.

Kwarleyz Group specialises in investments and developments, connecting land, design, construction, operation and asset management — from Accra’s skyline to an industrial vision for the Western Region.

The group is the holding company for five companies spanning hospitality, industrial development, technology, construction and community investment. Its operating portfolio runs three hotels and serviced residences in Accra today, while its development programme — shown separately, never blended in — reaches toward a 2,000-acre industrial city at Takoradi.

05 — The Vision · Western Region

Petronia
City

Development concept imagery published by Kwarleyz Group — not a photograph of a built city

A planned 2,000-acre industrial hub at Takoradi, in Ghana’s Western Region — conceived as the first fully integrated business hub for West Africa’s oil, gas and mining industries, inside a Free Zone enclave.

Petronia brings manufacturing, energy, digital infrastructure and cargo logistics into one development plan. Land is secured for the industrial hub, with site infrastructure progressing, per the group.
