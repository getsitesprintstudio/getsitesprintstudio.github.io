01 · The problem

One form stands between your couples and 2028.

La Dogana hosts a limited number of weddings a year, and reservations can be made up to two years in advance. The 2027 season sold out completely. On 1 December, a year of 2028 dates opens at once — and today, the only thing receiving them is a web form and an inbox.

i.

A whole season, one morning

Every 2028 date opens on the same day. The couples who enquire first expect an answer first — a form cannot keep that order.

ii.

A list with no sequence

The priority waiting list has no positions, no references and no date holds. Nobody can be told where they stand, and no date can be held while a couple decides.

iii.

Every answer written by hand

Enquiries are answered one by one — the estate replies within two business days — while couples choosing between venues decide in hours.

iv.

The apartments wait too

Eleven apartments and two houses, and no rates or availability published anywhere. Every stay, like every wedding, begins as an email and a wait.

04 · The wedding itself

Four days, the whole estate yours

The 2028 package, exactly as the estate publishes it: a complete four-day experience with full wedding planning, exclusive use, and every meal from the welcome night to the final dinner.

1

Arrival & Welcome Night

Guests settle into their apartments from 2 pm. In the evening, a buffet dinner under the string lights — homemade pasta, focaccia, pinsa and gelato, with wine, beer and aperitivo included.

2

The Wedding Day

A symbolic ceremony on the Roman terrace among the olive trees, aperitivo by the pool, a seated dinner tailored to your guests, then cake cutting and three hours of open bar and dancing, with music until 2 am.

3

Pool Brunch & Last Dinner

A relaxed poolside brunch above the lake, and a final dinner of bruschette with the estate's own organic olive oil, cured meats and cheeses, homemade lasagna and tiramisù.

4

Farewell & Check-out

Breakfast, a last swim or a walk among the olive trees — then goodbyes, or the start of your guests' Italian holiday.
