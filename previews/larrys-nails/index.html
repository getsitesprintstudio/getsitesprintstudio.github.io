Twenty years of good nails

Larry has been doing nails for over 20 years, and it shows in the details — sets that last, shapes that hold, and regulars who won’t sit in anyone else’s chair. Ask for Larry or Mimi: they’re the names Clarksville clients ask for by name, year after year.

The salon itself is part of the draw — a wide-open floor with rows of manicure stations, a full polish wall, spa pedicure thrones, and a spot little guests talk about all week: pink butterfly pedicure chairs made just for kids, so a mother-daughter pedicure actually feels like an occasion.

Come for a full spa pedicure ritual, a fresh dip set, or nail art for a night out. One visit, and you’ll understand why clients say their nails last longer after Larry’s.
