Good to know

Questions, answered the ranch's way

Every answer comes from Mountain Sky's own published pages.

How do deposits work?

New reservations require a 25% deposit at the time of booking and an additional 25% deposit due February 1st, 2027. If the reservation is made after January 1st, a 50% deposit is due at the time of booking. The remaining package price, along with any charges accrued during your stay, is due at checkout. All deposits are nonrefundable.

What is included in the package rates?

Lodging, meals, and on-ranch activities — horseback riding, the kids program, golf, guided hiking, yoga, and fly fishing instruction — plus use of all ranch facilities and amenities: heated pool, hot tub, grotto, steam room, sauna, and tennis court. Wild West Women also includes a tour of Yellowstone National Park and airport transportation.

What costs extra?

An 8% Montana Lodging Facility Sales and Use Tax on the lodging portion of your stay, and a 9% service charge (11% on buyouts). Depending on the package: airport transportation, massage services, bar and gift shop purchases, clay shooting, and off-ranch excursions such as Yellowstone National Park tours and whitewater rafting.

How long is a stay?

Summer Family Season and Fall Roundup are one-week stays only, Sunday to Sunday (7 nights). Wild West Adventures and Wild West Women are 5-night stays, Tuesday to Sunday. Ranch buyouts run 3, 5 or 7 nights with a three-night minimum.

Can children ride?

For safety, the ranch does not provide trail riding for children six years and under, though they can lead-line ride daily in the kids program. Babysitting is available for all ages outside of Kids Program hours, at $25–$35 per hour depending on time of day.

How do we get there?

The ranch is in Paradise Valley, about 1 hour 15 minutes from Bozeman Airport and 40 minutes from the North Entrance to Yellowstone. Round-trip transportation from Bozeman is available for $375 per party in one shuttle.

Can we bring our dog?

Pets are not permitted at the ranch.

What payment methods does the ranch accept?

All major credit cards, personal checks, and wire transfers.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Due to Montana liquor license laws, you are not permitted to BYOB inside the lodge, though you may keep personal alcohol in your cabin.
