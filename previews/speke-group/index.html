01 — Why this platform exists

The group is world-class.
The booking path is not.

Speke Group runs Uganda's most complete hospitality portfolio — a lakeside resort, a convention centre, a heritage hotel and ten more addresses. Yet a guest who wants to book today is sent sideways: to third-party portals that take a cut, to a festive post that ends in a phone call, or to a different number on every property page.

a.

Bookings leak to portals

Guests find Speke properties on third-party booking sites first — and the group pays commission on its own rooms.

b.

Festive bookings close by hand

The group's festive campaigns end with "call or WhatsApp" — every enquiry handled manually, with no structured details attached.

c.

Five-plus numbers, no single desk

Different contact numbers appear across the group's own pages, so guests guess — and enquiries scatter.

The fix — this page

One platform. One desk. One number.

  • All 13 properties presented properly, in one collection
  • A guided booking flow that collects property, dates, guests, rooms and guest details — before anyone picks up a phone
  • Every request arrives at the reservations desk as one complete, structured WhatsApp message
  • Festive and conference enquiries run through the same desk, in the same format
  • The number is the one Speke Resort Munyonyo already publishes: +256 752 711 714
