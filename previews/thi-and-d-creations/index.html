How to order

Ordering runs through WhatsApp - the way Thi&D Creations already works.

1

Pick your item number

Browse the catalogue above and note the item number, like B01 or V02.

2

Tap Order

The button opens WhatsApp with the item number, name and price already written for you.

3

Make it yours

Customisation is offered - ask about colours and personal touches in the same chat.

4

Pickup or delivery

Pick up at 219 Willis Street, Wellington, or ask about NZ-wide delivery.
