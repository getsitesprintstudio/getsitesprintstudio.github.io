What can you request?

If it is on your roof, it starts with a free inspection.

Roof repair

Missing or curled shingles, flashing problems, vents and boots, small sections that have seen better days.

Leaks

A stain on the ceiling or a drip in the attic usually means water is already getting in. Sooner is cheaper than later.

Storm damage

Wind and hail damage is easy to miss from the ground. An inspection documents what the storm actually did to your roof.

Full replacement

When a roof is at the end of its life, get a straight quote on a new one — shingle or metal — before leaks force the decision.

Inspections

Buying, selling, or just want to know where your roof stands? A free inspection gives you the answer in writing.
