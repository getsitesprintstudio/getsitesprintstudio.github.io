Piece A — Perps in a self-custody wallet (7 tweets)

A1

Most perps traders hand their keys to an exchange and hope. @KriptoK flips that: it's a self-custody wallet with perpetuals built in — you trade, but your keys and assets stay yours. A quick perps refresher, and why the custody part matters 🧵

A2

A perpetual future is a bet on an asset's price with no expiry date. Long = you profit if price rises, short = if it falls. Instead of settlement dates, a "funding rate" paid between longs and shorts keeps the perp price anchored to spot.

A3

Leverage magnifies everything. At 10x, roughly a 10% move against you can wipe the position — that's liquidation. Sane perps trading = small size, an exit plan, and never treating leverage as free money. The instrument isn't the risk; position size is.

A4

On a centralized exchange, your balance is an IOU. Withdrawals can pause and accounts can freeze. In a self-custody wallet like @KriptoK, assets live on-chain under your recovery phrase — lose the phrase and nobody, not even KriptoK, can restore it.

A5

KriptoK supports 13 networks (Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Sui and more), with best-route swaps across chains. Its wallet security model was audited by Halborn, including a full white-box penetration test. Verify, don't trust.

A6

It also goes beyond crypto perps: tokenized stocks and trending tokens sit in the same wallet, and through partner Collector Crypt even tokenized physical collectibles like graded Pokémon cards. One app to hold, swap and trade — not five exchange accounts.

A7

The honest caveats: perps liquidate people every day, funding costs add up on crowded trades, and self-custody means YOU are the security team. Start small, back up your phrase offline, and size like you plan to survive. Not financial advice.

Piece B — How to qualify for a KriptoK League round (7 tweets)

B1

How qualifying for a @KriptoKLeague round actually works — it's simpler than most trading competitions, but two or three details will trip you up if you miss them. Walkthrough 🧵 @KriptoK

B2

Step 1: Set up the KriptoK wallet and back up your recovery phrase offline. It's self-custody — no phrase, no recovery, for anyone. Then fund a perps account; funding is possible from 9 networks, including Solana, Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum.

B3

Step 2: Get $200+ into your PERPS account specifically. Money sitting elsewhere in the wallet doesn't count toward the minimum. And depositing alone doesn't enter you — you still need filled orders.

B4

Step 3: Place orders. You activate the moment a KriptoK order fills while your perps equity is $200+. That fill counts as order #1, and you need 2+ filled orders to qualify. Multiple partial fills from the same order still count as one order.

B5

From there you're ranked live by % ROI — not account size. A $200 account and a $20,000 account compete purely on percentage return. The Top 5 split $2,000 every two-week round: $700 / $500 / $350 / $250 / $200.

B6

Rules that catch people out: dropping below $200 through trading losses does NOT disqualify you — but every withdrawal must leave at least $200 in the perps account, or you're out of the round. Winners get USDC in their KriptoK wallet within hours of on-chain verification.

B7

Points also stack across a 6-round season (~3 months): 25 points for 1st in a round down to 1 point just for qualifying. The season podium pays $10,000 / $3,000 / $1,000. Honest note: that $200 is real money in leveraged markets — only play with what you can afford to lose.

Piece C — Why the League's design is interesting (3 tweets)

C1

The most interesting design choice in @KriptoKLeague: ranking by % ROI instead of raw PnL or volume. Volume competitions are whale games. An ROI board at least lets a small, disciplined trader outrank a big, sloppy one. @KriptoK

C2

The fine print is smarter than usual too. Results are verified on-chain, round prizes are fixed ($700 for 1st), and deposits count as capital in the ROI math — so you can't juice your percentage by shrinking the base. Withdrawing mid-round actually disadvantages you.

C3

The honest downside: % ROI boards reward swinging hard, and perps punish swinging hard. That $200 minimum is real skin in a leveraged game. Treat the League as paid practice with real risk — over a 6-round season, boring consistency probably beats heroics. Not financial advice.
